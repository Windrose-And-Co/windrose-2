## 📌 Overview

Windrose & Company is a strategic consulting and technology firm that specializes in **business process optimization, AI-driven solutions, and digital transformation**. This website serves as a **central hub for clients and partners**, offering insights into **who we are, what we do, and how to connect with us**.

Built with **Bootstrap 5**, this **responsive, modern, and performance-optimized** landing page provides a professional online presence that reflects our commitment to excellence.

---

## ✨ Features

✅ **Elegant, Modern UI** – Designed with Bootstrap 5 for a seamless user experience  
✅ **Mobile Responsive** – Optimized for all screen sizes and devices  
✅ **Lightweight & Fast** – Minimal dependencies for optimal load times  
✅ **SEO & OpenGraph Ready** – Optimized meta tags for social sharing  
✅ **Integrated Contact & Booking Links** – Seamless scheduling with Calendly  
✅ **Social Media & GitHub Links** – Connect with us directly  
✅ **Google Maps Integration** – Easy location access for in-person visits
